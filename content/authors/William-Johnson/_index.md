William Johnson is a postdoctoral researcher who joined the Genetic Logic Lab
in the Fall of 2026 and is advised by Dr. Chris Myers. He is a Fellow in the
Intelligence Community Postdoctoral program and is working in the area of
biosurveillance for genetically engineered microbes. His fellowship research
is directed towards developing _GEMminer_, a suite of software tools for
detecting sequences associated with genetic engineering in metagenome
repositories.

Before joining the lab, William received a Ph.D in Environmental Engineering
at CU Boulder, where his work focused on
environmental surveillance for extreme hazards, including SARS-CoV-2 and
chemical residues associated with urban wildfires.
